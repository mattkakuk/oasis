conversation, that knot in your stomach is normal. Anxiety, in small doses,
can even sharpen your focus. But what happens when anxiety starts being the
lens through which you see everything?

For many, anxiety quietly takes the wheel without them realizing it. If life
is starting to feel smaller, here are five signs that anxiety may be running
your life.

## 1\. You Can’t Turn Your Brain Off at Night

Racing thoughts at bedtime are a hallmark of an overactive nervous system.
Your brain stays in "on" mode, scanning for threats even when you’re safe in
bed. Over time, this exhaustion impacts your patience and clarity.

## 2\. You Avoid Things That Make You Uncomfortable

Anxiety disguises avoidance as "common sense." You skip the party because
you're "tired" or put off the hard conversation because it's "not the right
time." The problem? Avoidance feeds anxiety, making the task feel even scarier
the next time.

## 3\. Your Body Is Always Braced for Something

From tight shoulders to a clenched jaw, anxiety lives in your muscles. Many
people treat the physical symptoms for years without addressing the root
cause. If you're feeling the physical toll of stress, our [anxiety therapists
in West Palm Beach](https://www.google.com/maps?cid=16104639230576114756)
specialize in resetting the nervous system through evidence-based care.

## 4\. You Need Constant Reassurance

Replaying conversations to look for hidden meanings or mentally rehearsing
disasters are two sides of the same coin: a mind that doesn't feel safe
trusting that things will be okay.

## 5\. Anxiety Is Affecting Your Relationships

Anxiety makes vulnerability feel dangerous. You might be irritable, pull away
from loved ones, or cling too tightly to feel secure. Relationships take the
hit because they are where we are most vulnerable. You can find resources for
healing these connections at our [counseling center in Stuart,
FL](https://www.google.com/maps?cid=17645624106782271069).
